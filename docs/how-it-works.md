# How it works

You do not need to know any of this to use the plugin. It is here for anyone who wants to know why the battery is doing what it is doing. The **Decision Reason** on the **Battery Manager** device always says the same thing in a sentence.

The plugin has one aim above the rest: buy as little from the grid as it can. Money comes second. When it has to choose between keeping energy in the battery and selling it, it keeps it, unless selling is clearly worth more once the losses of charging and discharging are counted.
